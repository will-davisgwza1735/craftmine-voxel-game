## Overview

CraftMine brings Minecraft-style voxel gameplay to the browser without requiring a traditional installation. The game is delivered as a standalone HTML file, making it straightforward to open, share, and run.

Its playable content includes 46 different block types, 36 mobs, and 19 weapons. Multiplayer functionality is also available for players who want to use the voxel world with others.

## Playing CraftMine

To begin a session:

1. Open the CraftMine HTML file in your browser.
2. Move through the voxel environment and interact with its blocks.
3. Encounter mobs and make use of the available weapons.
4. Choose multiplayer when you want to play with others.
5. Reload or reopen the page when you want to begin again.

CraftMine is operated through its browser interface; it is not intended as a command-line application.
